Algorithm TowerOfHanoi(n, Source, Auxiliary, Destination)

Step 1: If n = 1
            Print "Move disk 1 from Source to Destination"
            Return

Step 2: Call TowerOfHanoi(n-1, Source, Destination, Auxiliary)

Step 3: Print "Move disk n from Source to Destination"

Step 4: Call TowerOfHanoi(n-1, Auxiliary, Source, Destination)

Step 5: End
